# AI placeholder voices

These clips were generated by `tools/voice-gen/generate.py` with [Kokoro](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx)
(Kokoro-82M, open weights under the Apache-2.0 licence) — no real person's voice was cloned.
They stand in until real recordings exist: a clip in `assets/voice/<voice>/` always wins over the one here.

Limits: Kokoro has British and American English voices, not South African ones, so the accent is approximate.
Afrikaans words use espeak's Afrikaans pronunciation; isiXhosa words borrow Swahili rules (vowels are close,
clicks are missing). Have a native speaker listen before relying on them.
